Workday Inventory connects receiving, stock movements, and financial records. This prototype explores how visual evidence could help a receiving team review a delivery. A barcode identifies the item. A short video adds a view of its packaging and handling.

In this clip, the carton is opened. Jev Omni evaluates four possible observations directly, and ranks opening first. The percentages compare those answers; the source stays beside the result.

Now we add the business context. The same photo could belong to an authorized contents inspection, where an open box is expected. Or it could be submitted as proof of the package's arrival condition. In that case, our example rule asks for an earlier photo, before the box was opened.

The prototype keeps the selected action, the supplied rule, and the original evidence together. A receiver can review the packet and request the missing view before continuing the receiving process.

Here is how the technology works. TypeSafe's original Jev turns text and structured questions into typed decisions. Jev Omni is an independent open model with a similar decision interface and multimodal inputs.

Our video path samples sixteen frames. Those images enter a vision encoder, then a shared language backbone processes them with the question, context, and candidate answers. A classification head scores the options in one forward pass. Softmax converts those scores into the percentages we display. This avoids generating an answer word by word. The result is easy for software to route and for a reviewer to inspect.

The important evaluation question is whether that decision is supported by the evidence. In this restricted crop, the model gave closed carton eighty percent, although the full closure was out of view. A strong score can still accompany weak evidence. That case stays in the test set.

The seven-second clip took about three point three seconds, including preprocessing and local GPU inference. Image calls took around a quarter of a second.

For a receiving workflow, the opportunity is a compact evidence check: identify the item, inspect the visible condition, apply the capture context, and give the receiver a clear next step. The learning repository includes the prompts, recorded results, and this review prototype so others can explore the same questions.
